
### 1. Brand Identity & Voice

Daghri translates to "straightaway" or "directly." The brand must consistently project speed, reliability, and local Palestinian pride.

* **Mission:** To empower local commerce by providing the fastest, most dependable delivery service across Palestine.
* **Tone of Voice:** Friendly, urgent yet reassuring, and authentic. Communication should be straightforward, avoiding complex corporate jargon, and should use familiar, culturally relevant phrasing.

---

### 2. Logo System & Usage

The logo is the most recognizable asset of Daghri, combining modern delivery iconography with bilingual typography.

* **Primary Logo:** The horizontal lockup combining the courier illustration, the Arabic script (دُغْري), and the English text (DAGHRI). This is the preferred version for most applications.
* **Clear Space:** Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the Arabic letter "د" (Dal) in the wordmark to ensure it stands out.
* **Improper Usage:** Never stretch, distort, or rotate the logo. Do not alter the official colors or place the full-color logo over cluttered photographic backgrounds.

---

### 3. Color Palette & Typography

Our visual system relies on energetic colors and highly legible typography to communicate efficiency.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Primary Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Daghri Orange | `#FF6B4E` | App headers, primary buttons, rider uniforms, vehicle branding. |
| **Secondary** | Trust Teal | `#26A69A` | Accent graphics, secondary UI elements, trust badges. |
| **Tertiary** | Stability Navy | `#1A2B45` | Typography, footers, high-contrast structural elements. |
| **Accent** | Growth Green | `#4CAF50` | Success notifications, order tracking milestones. |

* **Typography:** Use custom dynamic fonts for primary headings to convey motion (like *Daghri Sans*). For body text, use a clean, modern geometric sans-serif (like *Poppins*).
* **Hierarchy:** Keep Arabic and English font weights balanced. Headings should be bold and prominent, while body copy must prioritize readability on mobile screens.

---

### 4. Imagery & Visual Elements

Daghri’s imagery must reflect the reality and beauty of its operating environment while highlighting top-tier service.

* **Photography:** Feature warm, candid photos of friendly couriers interacting with smiling customers. Ensure backgrounds feature authentic, recognizable Palestinian streets and architecture.
* **Brand Patterns:** Utilize modern interpretations of traditional Palestinian *Tatreez* (embroidery) patterns as subtle background textures, banner borders, or framing elements on promotional flyers.
